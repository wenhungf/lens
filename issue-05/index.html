Can’t chew it? Make congee.咬不動？那就煮粥。

It’s what most families try first. It fixes less than it seems.大多數家庭第一個想到的就是這招。可是它解決的問題，比看起來少。

Grandma Chen knows a balanced diet matters. But the meat feels too hard and the vegetables too tough, so she cooks congee for every meal, with a salted egg and some pickles. She worries about wasting food, so she keeps it simple. The Health Promotion Administration (HPA) opens its handbook on food for older people with her story.陳奶奶知道吃得均衡很重要。可是肉太硬、菜太老，她就餐餐煮稀飯，配鹹蛋和醬菜。煮太多又怕浪費，乾脆吃得簡單一點。國民健康署的高齡飲食手冊，開頭講的就是她的故事。

About two in three Taiwanese aged 65 and over say their teeth, chewing or swallowing limit the kinds of food they eat. And there are more of them every year. At the end of 2025, 4.67 million people in Taiwan were 65 or older, 20.06% of the population. By the World Health Organization’s definition, Taiwan is now a super-aged society.65 歲以上的國人，每 3 人就有 2 人說，牙齒、咀嚼或吞嚥的問題讓他們少吃了某些種類的食物。這群人還一年比一年多。到 2025 年底，台灣 65 歲以上的人口有 467 萬，占總人口 20.06%。照世界衛生組織的定義，台灣已經是超高齡社會。

The magic number神奇數字03 / 08

Count the teeth.數數牙齒。

Dentists use twenty as the line. Japan has been chasing it since 1989.牙醫把二十顆當成一條分界。日本從 1989 年就開始追這個數字。

Japan: 80-year-olds with 20 or more natural teeth日本：保有 20 顆以上自然牙的 80 歲長者

Percent of people aged 80. 1989 is an estimate from the start of the 8020 campaign; later years are estimated from ages 75–84. Source: Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, Survey of Dental Diseases.80 歲長者的百分比。1989 年是 8020 運動剛開始時的估計值；之後各年由 75–84 歲推估。資料來源：日本厚生勞動省「齒科疾病實態調查」。

Taiwan, 2023: natural teeth, on average台灣 2023 年：平均自然牙顆數

One tooth = one natural tooth, out of a full set of 32. More than half of people 75 and over have fewer than 20. Source: Ministry of Health and Welfare, 2021–2023 oral health survey.一顆牙代表一顆自然牙，全口共 32 顆。75 歲以上的人，超過一半不到 20 顆。資料來源：衛福部 110–112 年成人及老人口腔健康調查。

With about twenty natural teeth, most people can still chew ordinary food. That’s why Japan’s health ministry and dentists started the 8020 campaign in 1989: twenty teeth at age eighty. At the start, only about one 80-year-old in fourteen made it. In 2024, six in ten did.大約有二十顆自然牙，大多數人就還能咬一般的食物。所以日本的厚生省和牙醫在 1989 年發起「8020 運動」：80 歲還有 20 顆牙。剛開始時，80 歲的長者大約每十四人才有一人做到。到了 2024 年，已經是每十人有六人。

Taiwan counted teeth too, in its 2023 national survey. People aged 65 to 74 kept about 21 natural teeth on average. People 75 and over kept about 16, and more than half had fewer than twenty. About one in nine people over 65 had no natural teeth at all. The government’s goal is an average of twenty for people over 65 by 2026.台灣在 2023 年的全國調查裡也數了牙齒。65 到 74 歲的人，平均還有大約 21 顆自然牙；75 歲以上平均大約 16 顆，而且超過一半不到二十顆。65 歲以上的人，大約每九人就有一人一顆自然牙都不剩。政府的目標，是 2026 年讓 65 歲以上的人平均有二十顆。

The Hangzhou study in the video found the same line: residents with twenty or more natural teeth ate a wider range of foods. But twenty is only a count. What happens when the missing teeth are replaced?影片裡的杭州研究也看到同一條分界：自然牙有二十顆以上的住民，吃的種類比較多。不過二十只是一個數字。少掉的牙補上之後，又會怎樣？

What counts as a tooth?怎樣才算一顆牙？

Taiwan’s survey counts up to 32 natural teeth. A tooth with a crown still counts, as long as its root is your own. Removable dentures, bridges and implants don’t.台灣的調查最多算 32 顆自然牙。套了牙套的牙，只要牙根是自己的，就算數；活動假牙、牙橋和植牙都不算。

At the dentist在牙醫診所04 / 08

Teeth you can take out.可以拿下來的牙。

Dentures keep people eating normally. Who pays for them just changed.假牙讓人能照常吃飯。誰來付錢，今年剛換了。

Two kinds of denture兩種假牙

Fixed固定假牙

Cemented to your own teeth or roots; stays in.黏在自己的牙齒或牙根上，不拿下來。

Removable活動假牙

Rests on the gums; you can take it out.靠在牙齦上，可以拿下來。

Lose your back teeth and you lose the part of your mouth that grinds. Dentures fill the gap, and they help. In the video’s Japanese care-home study, residents who wore dentures had 2.82 times the odds of staying on a regular diet.後牙掉了，嘴裡負責磨碎食物的地方也就沒了。假牙可以補上這個空缺，也真的有用。影片裡的日本長照機構研究發現，有戴假牙的住民，維持一般飲食的勝算是沒戴的 2.82 倍。

But a denture you can take out doesn’t chew like a tooth. A fixed denture is cemented in place. A removable one rests on the gums. In the Serbian study, people who chewed with removable dentures did about as well as people with no back teeth meeting at all. People with natural teeth or fixed dentures chewed far better.不過，能拿下來的假牙，咬起東西來不像真牙。固定假牙是黏死的，活動假牙則靠在牙齦上。塞爾維亞的研究發現，靠活動假牙咀嚼的人，表現跟完全沒有後牙對咬的人差不多。天然牙或固定假牙的人，咬得好多了。

Dentures cost money, and the help just changed. From 2009, the central government paid for dentures for low-income seniors; by mid-2025, more than 88,000 had them. In 2026, the program moved to city and county governments, which now set their own rules. Some help only low-income seniors. Some now help everyone over 65.假牙要花錢，補助今年也變了。從 2009 年起，中低收入長者的假牙由中央出錢補助；到 2025 年中，已經有超過 8.8 萬人裝了假牙。2026 年起，這項補助交給各縣市自己辦，規定由地方決定。有的縣市只補助中低收入長者，有的現在 65 歲以上都能申請。

Check your city查查妳住的縣市

Rules and amounts now depend on where your grandparents live. Search the name of their city or county with 老人假牙補助, or call the local social affairs bureau. A dentist has to check their mouth before they apply.現在的規定和金額，要看長輩住在哪裡。用縣市名稱加上「老人假牙補助」搜尋，或打電話問當地的社會局。申請之前，要先給牙醫師檢查口腔。

Lunch, again又是午餐05 / 08

The shrinking plate.縮水的餐盤。

Nobody decides to stop eating fruit. It just gets harder, one meal at a time.沒有人會決定從此不吃水果。只是一餐一餐，變得越來越難吃下去。

My Plate, before and after我的餐盤：之前與之後

The HPA’s six food groups, with the groups that shrink when chewing gets hard (Kim 2023): grains, fruit, protein, dairy. Only fruit’s drop, about a quarter, is drawn to scale.國健署的六大類食物，咀嚼困難時會縮水的類別（Kim 2023）：全穀雜糧、水果、豆魚蛋肉、乳品。只有水果少掉的量（大約四分之一）是照比例畫的。

When chewing gets hard, the plate changes quietly. In the video’s Korean study of 7,835 older adults, people who found chewing hard ate less grain, potato, fruit, meat and dairy. Fresh fruit dropped by about a quarter.咀嚼一變困難，餐盤就悄悄地變了。影片裡那項 7,835 位韓國長者的研究發現，覺得咀嚼困難的人，穀類、薯類、水果、肉類和乳品都吃得比較少，新鮮水果少了大約四分之一。

Taiwan’s numbers look much the same. In the national nutrition survey of 2014 to 2017, eight or nine in ten people over 65 had less than one serving of dairy a day, and less than two servings of fruit. The HPA says many older people also fall short on protein, fiber, calcium, magnesium and vitamin D.台灣的數字也差不多。2014 到 2017 年的國民營養調查裡，65 歲以上的人有八到九成，每天乳品不到一份，水果不到兩份。國健署也說，很多長者的蛋白質、膳食纖維、鈣、鎂和維生素 D 都吃不夠。

So softness is only half the job. In the Hangzhou study, more teeth went with a more varied diet, and a more varied diet went with better nutrition. The HPA’s answer is a plate with six parts, and a hand sign for each so they’re easy to remember.所以，煮軟只做到一半。杭州的研究發現，牙齒多的人飲食比較多樣，飲食多樣的人營養比較好。國健署的做法是「我的餐盤」：一個盤子分成六類，每一類都配一個手勢，方便記。

My Plate, in six hand signs我的餐盤，六個手勢

  1. A cup of milk morning and evening每天早晚一杯奶
  2. Fruit the size of your fist at every meal每餐水果拳頭大
  3. A little more vegetables than fruit菜比水果多一點
  4. As much rice as vegetables飯跟蔬菜一樣多
  5. Beans, fish, eggs or meat the size of your palm豆魚蛋肉一掌心
  6. A teaspoon of nuts and seeds堅果種子一茶匙

In the kitchen在廚房06 / 08

Soften the food, not the menu.改的是軟硬，不是菜單。

Taiwan sorts soft food into levels. The first two are for people who chew poorly but swallow well.台灣把軟質食物分成好幾級。前兩級，是給咀嚼不好、但吞嚥沒問題的人。

LevelEasy to bite容易咬軟質食

Cut under 3 cm · like black dried tofu and apple切成 3 公分以下・像黑豆干、蘋果

LevelGum-mashable牙齦碎軟質食

Cut under 1.5 cm · like firm tofu and banana切成 1.5 公分以下的丁・像板豆腐、香蕉

The HPA’s handbook sorts soft food into levels and tests each one with chopsticks, a spoon and a fork. “Easy to bite” food is soft but still needs some chewing, like black dried tofu or apple; cut it into pieces under 3 cm. “Gum-mashable” food breaks under the gums alone, like firm tofu or banana; cut it into cubes under 1.5 cm.國健署的手冊把軟質食物分級，用筷子、湯匙和叉子就能測。「容易咬軟質食」夠軟，但還是要嚼一下，像黑豆干、蘋果，切成 3 公分以下就好。「牙齦碎軟質食」用牙齦就能壓碎，像板豆腐、香蕉，要切成 1.5 公分以下的丁。

The handbook’s tricks change how food is cooked, not what’s on the menu. Soak brown rice, keep it in the fridge for a day, then in the freezer for a day before you cook it. Rub meat with fresh pineapple juice to make it tender. (Canned pineapple won’t work.) Freeze tough vegetables like bell peppers for one to three days, then cook them straight from the freezer.手冊裡的小撇步，改的是煮法，不是菜色。糙米泡水後冷藏一天、再冷凍一天，才下鍋煮。肉用新鮮鳳梨汁醃過會變嫩（鳳梨罐頭沒有效）。甜椒這類纖維粗的蔬菜，冷凍一到三天，不用退冰就直接煮。

In the handbook, Grandpa Chen can still chew, just not hard or big pieces, so his doctor suggests easy-to-bite food. Grandma Chen can’t chew with her teeth, but her gums and tongue still work, so hers is gum-mashable. Their dietitian plans a full day of meals for each of them. Now it’s your turn.在手冊裡，陳爺爺還能嚼，只是太硬、太大塊的不行，所以醫師建議他吃「容易咬」的食物。陳奶奶的牙齒咬不動，但牙齦和舌頭還能幫忙，所以她適合「牙齦碎」。營養師替兩人各排了一天的三餐。現在換妳來。

Pack a bento幫長輩裝便當

The softer dishes, the levels and Grandpa and Grandma Chen come from the HPA’s 高齡營養飲食質地衛教手冊. A game, not a meal plan: for real eating problems, ask a dietitian.較軟的菜色、分級和陳爺爺、陳奶奶，都出自國健署《高齡營養飲食質地衛教手冊》。這是遊戲，不是菜單；真的有進食困難，請找營養師。

Dinner is served開飯了07 / 08

Soft doesn’t have to look like baby food.軟，不一定要像嬰兒食品。

When food is blended smooth, people eat with their eyes first. Shaping it back helps.食物打成泥以後，人會先用眼睛吃。把形狀做回來，就有幫助。

Shaped purée塑形的泥 Plain purée一坨泥

When chewing or swallowing gets very hard, food is often blended into a purée. It’s safe to swallow, but residents and staff in aged care describe it as baby food, or as “slop”. And as the Singapore study in the video found, people with chewing problems like the same foods as everyone else.咀嚼或吞嚥變得很困難時，食物常常會被打成泥。這樣吞起來安全，但長照機構的住民和員工都說，它看起來像嬰兒食品，甚至像餿水。而影片裡的新加坡研究發現，咀嚼有困難的人，喜歡的食物跟其他人差不多。

A 2023 review of 18 studies looked at food shaping: pressing purées into molds, piping them through a bag, or 3D printing them, so the chicken looks like chicken again. Older adults were more satisfied with molded purées than plain ones, and some studies found people ate more. Shape alone isn’t enough, though. The food still has to taste good, and a sauce helps.2023 年一篇回顧整理了 18 項研究，看的是食物塑形：把泥狀食物壓進模具、用擠花袋擠出來，或用 3D 列印，讓雞肉看起來又像雞肉。比起一坨泥，長者對塑形過的泥狀食物比較滿意，也有幾項研究發現他們吃得比較多。不過光有形狀不夠，味道還是要好，淋點醬汁也有幫助。

Taiwan is working on it too. Over three years, the HPA has trained 1,688 food workers and 500 health professionals to cook texture-adjusted meals, and in 2024 it held its first cooking competition for dishes older people can bite. One HPA recipe is a zongzi with no sticky rice at all: fish and shrimp paste, shaped like the real thing.台灣也在做。三年來，國健署培訓了 1,688 位餐飲從業人員和 500 位專業人員，學做質地調整的餐點，2024 年還第一次舉辦了料理競賽，比的是長輩咬得動的菜。國健署有一道食譜是不用糯米的粽子：用魚漿和蝦漿，做成粽子的樣子。

Five ways to shape soft food塑形的五種工具

A spoon, for round or oval scoops. An ice-cream scoop. A food mold. A piping bag. A 3D food printer. The first four are cheap. Printers cost much more, and they haven’t yet been tested in clinical trials with older people.湯匙，舀成圓形或橢圓形；冰淇淋勺；食物模具；擠花袋；還有 3D 食物列印機。前四種都很便宜，列印機貴得多，也還沒在長者身上做過臨床試驗。

Your turn換妳了08 / 08

Why do you eat what you eat?妳為什麼吃妳吃的東西？

Teeth are one reason. Researchers count fifteen.牙齒是其中一個原因。研究人員一共數出十五個。

Tap your top three點選妳的前三名

Why did you choose lunch today? Researchers count fifteen eating motives, from liking and habit to price, health and how food looks. In 2025, a team in China translated their Eating Motivation Survey into Chinese and tested it with 249 older adults in Changsha.妳今天午餐為什麼吃那個？研究人員數出十五種進食動機，從喜好、習慣，到價格、健康和外觀都有。2025 年，中國的一個研究團隊把這份「進食動機量表」翻成中文，在長沙找了 249 位長者測試。

The older people tested the wording too. They found the print too small and some words hard, so the team made the font bigger, spaced the lines out and added examples next to difficult terms. And motives travel differently: in the Chinese sample, traditional eating stood out, while in Brazil, eating with other people mattered more.長者也幫忙試了題目的用字。他們覺得字太小、有些詞太難，研究團隊就把字放大、行距拉開，也在難懂的詞旁邊補上例子。不同地方的人，動機也不一樣：中國的樣本特別看重傳統飲食，巴西的人則更在意跟別人一起吃。

Your own reasons matter for your own mouth. The Taiwanese survey in the video (1,229 people aged 16 to 65) found that the more poor eating habits people had, the worse their oral health. Japan’s goal is twenty teeth at eighty, and the teeth you’ll chew with at eighty are the ones you have now.妳自己的理由，也關係到妳自己的牙齒。影片裡那份台灣調查（1,229 位 16 到 65 歲的民眾）發現，不良飲食習慣越多，口腔健康越差。日本的目標是 80 歲還有 20 顆牙，而妳 80 歲時用來咬東西的牙，就是妳現在嘴裡這些。
